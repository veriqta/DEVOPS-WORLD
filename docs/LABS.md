# Lab standard

Each lab must include: objective, audience, prerequisites, estimated time, cost warning, architecture, setup, commands with explanation, verification, expected output, deliberate failure, diagnosis, rollback, cleanup, knowledge checks with answers, rubric and portfolio evidence.

## Practical assessment rubric

- Functionality: 25 points
- Security: 20 points
- Reliability and recovery: 20 points
- Observability: 15 points
- Documentation and reproducibility: 10 points
- Cost and cleanup: 10 points

Pass threshold: 80/100 with no critical security failure.
